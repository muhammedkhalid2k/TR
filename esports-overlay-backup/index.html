What you get

  • Two themes — angular tactical look for CoD, glass/neon for FC. Change every colour from the panel, globally or per overlay.
  • Face cams — 8 player cams (CoD) or 2 (FC) with frames and name plates; hotkeys focus one cam and glide it to the bottom centre.
  • Casters & stage cams — desk shot and corner window; pick cams straight from your NDI network.
  • Light on vMix — a single browser input shows every scene and only builds what is on screen.

About this website

This page hosts the demo and the asset exporter. The live production setup runs on the vMix PC (start-server.bat): it keeps the control panel and vMix in sync, reads NDI cams and lets other PCs control the show. Those parts cannot run on a web host, so in the demo the panel and overlay tabs sync inside your own browser only.
